What is Food Waste?

Food waste occurs when edible food is discarded or unused. This can happen at production, distribution, or consumption levels. Reducing food waste is critical for sustainability and combating hunger.

Examples of Food Waste

  • Unused or expired packaged foods.
  • Spoiled fruits and vegetables.
  • Leftover meals and scraps.
  • Overproduced or unsold bakery goods.
  • Discarded restaurant and cafeteria food.

Reducing food waste by 50% could feed over a billion people annually!
