DIY Composting at Home

Turn your kitchen scraps into nutrient-rich compost for your garden.

4 Simple Steps to Start Composting

Step 1: Choose a Spot

Select a dry, shady location for your compost bin or pile, ideally near a water source.

Step 2: Gather Organic Waste

Collect kitchen scraps like vegetable peels, fruit waste, and coffee grounds.

Step 3: Add Dry Materials

Mix in dry leaves, shredded newspaper, and cardboard to balance the compost.

Step 4: Turn the Pile

Regularly aerate your compost by turning it to speed up the decomposition process.
