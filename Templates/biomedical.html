What is Biomedical Waste?

Biomedical waste refers to waste generated during the diagnosis, treatment, or immunization of humans or animals. It includes infectious, hazardous, and non-hazardous materials requiring special handling and disposal.

Examples of Biomedical Waste

  • Infectious waste (used surgical dressings).
  • Pathological waste (human/animal tissues).
  • Sharps (needles, syringes, scalpels).
  • Pharmaceutical waste (expired medicines).
  • Chemical waste (disinfectants, reagents).

Hospitals worldwide generate over 2 million tons of biomedical waste daily, making proper waste management essential for safety and health!
