Types of Waste

01. BIODEGRADABLE WASTE

Biodegradable waste consists of organic matter that can be decomposed by microorganisms and other living organisms through processes like composting and digestion.

Know more

02. BIOMEDICAL WASTE

Biomedical waste includes any infectious or potentially infectious materials from the treatment of humans or animals and research involving biologics.

Know more

03. CHEMICAL WASTE

Chemical waste encompasses excess, unused, or unwanted chemicals.

Know more

04. ELECTRONIC WASTE

Electronic waste (e-waste) refers to discarded electrical or electronic devices, also known as waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) or end-of-life (EOL) electronics.

Know more

05. INDUSTRIAL WASTE

Industrial waste is the waste produced by industrial activity which includes any material that is rendered useless during a manufacturing process such as that of factories, mills, and mining operations.

Know more

06. AGRICULTURAL WASTE

Agricultural waste are plant residues from agriculture. These waste streams originate from arable land and horticulture.

Know more

07. FOOD WASTE

Food loss and waste refers to food that is not consumed. It occurs throughout the food system from production to consumption.
