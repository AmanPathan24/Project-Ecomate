What is Chemical Waste?

Chemical waste refers to any discarded material that contains hazardous chemicals or is generated as a byproduct of chemical processes. It requires proper handling to avoid harm to the environment and human health.

Examples of Chemical Waste

  • Expired or unused laboratory chemicals.
  • Solvents, acids, and bases from industrial processes.
  • Paints, varnishes, and thinners.
  • Pesticides and herbicides.
  • Heavy metal-containing waste (e.g., mercury, lead).

Properly recycling chemical solvents can save up to 90% of the energy required to produce new ones!
