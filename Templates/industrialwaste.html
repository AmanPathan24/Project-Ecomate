What is Industrial Waste?

Industrial waste refers to waste materials produced by manufacturing or industrial processes. It includes solid, liquid, and gaseous byproducts that require careful handling to prevent environmental damage.

Examples of Industrial Waste

  • Metal scraps from machining processes.
  • Industrial sludge and wastewater.
  • Non-biodegradable packaging materials.
  • Chemical byproducts and residues.
  • Excess or waste raw materials.

Recycling steel from industrial waste can save 75% of the energy required to produce new steel!
