What is Electronic Waste?

Electronic waste, or e-waste, includes discarded electronic devices such as computers, smartphones, and televisions. It contains hazardous components but also valuable materials that can be recycled.

Examples of Electronic Waste

  • Old computers, laptops, and smartphones.
  • Broken televisions and monitors.
  • Discarded printers and copiers.
  • Damaged electronic toys and appliances.
  • Batteries and chargers.

Recycling 1 million laptops saves the same amount of energy as powering 3,657 U.S. homes for a year!
